Temperature changes the trigger, not the ranking

One positive number divides every logit. Nobody's prediction changes, no ranking moves — and the queue of statements a human has to read is cut by two thirds. That gap is the lab.

Predict before you fit

Divide every logit by one positive temperature. Which of these can change — the ranking, accuracy at the 50% line, NLL, or the set of statements sent to an analyst at an 85% threshold?

Ranking and the 50% decision cannot change. Dividing by T > 0 preserves the order of the logits and fixes the sign, so the predicted label is untouched. NLL, Brier and ECE all move, because they score the probability. And a threshold above 50% can move the action set a long way.

What calibration cannot fix

Confidently wrong

Every cost row is gold-labelled unfavorable and scored strongly favorable. The scorer sees increased and a large number and reads good news; a rising cost is bad news.

Fitting T pushes those below 85% and they leave the queue — but they are still wrong, still ranked above statements that deserve the slot. Drag the threshold to 0.75 and they walk straight back in.
